# Card artwork and grading identifiers

Card search and artwork: https://tcgdex.dev/ and https://github.com/tcgdex/cards-database . API/database MIT (Copyright TCGdex); card artwork belongs to its respective rights holders. No SDK code copied or dependency installed. Exact selected identifiers and source URLs are retained in purchase JSON backups. Catalog artwork is not a photograph of the user's slab.

CGC identifier: official red SVG from https://www.cgcgrading.com/ header, https://images.ctfassets.net/t8bripszr2o9/1aWH36oCTDIjHeGhd3RNSZ/ceeffbea0246725e8b6efa88fd7a709a/cgc_red.svg . Bundled unchanged.

PSA identifier: original vector logo paths extracted from the top-left of the official submission PDF, https://assets.ctfassets.net/l40e281thfxr/1nUzpGUanzGDuO5O9WveP7/2f6620cf19394386b707012231d83000/PSADNA-Autograph-Encapsulation-Submission-Form.pdf . Shape, proportions and supplied color preserved; no form text or submission details included. Brand guidance reviewed at https://www.psacard.com/logo and https://app.collectors.com/trademarkpolicy .

PSA and CGC marks remain their owners' trademarks. Used only to identify the grader the user typed, alongside user-recorded grades. No affiliation, endorsement, automatic certification check or authenticity claim. https://www.cgccards.com/legal/terms-of-use/ reviewed. These assets do not become covered by the project or TCGdex code license.

Performance: 12 results per explicit search, no background catalog/price requests. Low WebP thumbnails are lazy-loaded; high WebP fetched on explicit viewing. Cache-first artwork storage capped at 180 objects and 24 MiB, separate from app shell and records. CORS-readable images only are persisted; malformed CORS images may display uncached. Browser storage eviction can remove artwork; saved card identities remain in the ledger and images can reload online. No physical-phone battery benchmark is claimed.
